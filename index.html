Common questions

A few things families in Watford & Borehamwood often ask before getting started.

How are your Care Professionals recruited and trained?+

Every carer goes through a thorough recruitment process, including full background and DBS checks, followed by induction training covering medication support, mobility, safeguarding and dementia care. We don't use agency staff — our team is directly employed and receives ongoing training and supervision.

Will we always see the same carer?+

Consistency matters, so we aim to match you with a small, familiar team rather than a rotating cast of strangers. If your usual carer is unavailable, we introduce someone from your small team who already knows your routine and care plan.

What if care needs change over time?+

We talk any changes through with you and your family, involve relevant health professionals where needed, and adjust visit times, support levels or introduce overnight care as appropriate — all managed by our office team, so you don't have to coordinate it yourself.

Can I change or cancel a visit?+

Yes. We ask for reasonable notice, but we understand plans change and will do our best to stay flexible around you.

What rating has the CQC given Watford & Borehamwood?+

Our Watford & Borehamwood service registered with the CQC on 19 August 2024 and has not yet received its first inspection. We're happy to talk through what CQC regulation means in the meantime.

Not sure what kind of care you need?

That's okay. Tell us a little about your situation and our friendly local care team can help you understand your options.
